### Overview
AutoBookmark ("we", "our", or "extension") is developed by sinary.org. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when using the AutoBookmark browser extension.

### Data Collection and Usage
**AutoBookmark does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or browsing activity.**

- **Bookmarks Permission (`bookmarks`)**:  
  AutoBookmark accesses your browser bookmarks solely to display them within the auto-hiding floating navigation bar and to allow you to search through your bookmarks locally. Your bookmark titles, URLs, and folder structures **never leave your device**.
  
- **Storage Permission (`storage`)**:  
  Used exclusively via Chrome's local storage API (`chrome.storage.sync` / `chrome.storage.local`) to persist your personal UI preferences (such as auto-hide delay, trigger distance, show/hide search, and display options). No external servers are involved.

- **Active Tab Permission (`activeTab`)**:  
  Used solely to communicate between the browser tab and the extension service worker to toggle or display the bookmark bar overlay on active web pages.

### Third-Party Services
AutoBookmark does not use analytics, tracking cookies, advertising networks, or any third-party telemetry services. All bookmark lookups and search filtering are performed 100% locally on your machine.
